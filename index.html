07 / How I work

How I handle difficult customer & vendor issues

Listen → clarify → investigate → act → confirm. The same method behind vendor escalations and content review queues — select a step to see what I actually do at each stage.

01 / LISTEN

Listen

Understand what the customer or vendor is actually trying to resolve before jumping into a solution.

  • Acknowledge the frustration first
  • Let them explain fully
  • Keep the tone calm and professional

The goal: Start from what they need, not what is fastest to answer.

02 / CLARIFY

Clarify

Find the real issue with one clear question at a time.

  • Billing or invoice confusion
  • Hardware or device problem
  • Order, rider, or delivery status
  • Account settings or store details

The goal: Name the actual problem before offering a solution.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Investigate

Look at the information available before deciding what action makes sense.

  • Account information
  • Order status
  • Billing details
  • Previous interactions
  • Device / technical issue

The goal: Find the actual problem before offering a solution.

04 / ACT

Act

Fix what can be fixed now, and route the rest to the right place.

  • Resolve immediately when possible
  • Cancel, correct, update, or retry
  • Coordinate with the right team
  • Follow security steps for sensitive data

The goal: Own the case until it moves forward.

05 / CONFIRM

Confirm

Close the loop clearly so nothing is left ambiguous.

  • Explain what was done
  • Explain what happens next
  • Check the vendor understood
  • Document the case

The goal: The customer knows what happened and what happens next.
