What differs

  • 1 · Current. One flat shape: teardrop with the N cut out to transparent, so the N always takes the background colour. Viewbox 0 0 512 512, with a backing tile. This is what is live now.
  • 2 · Alternative. Three stacked shapes — white teardrop #FFFFFF, black circle #141414, white N #FFFFFF. The circle gives the N a ground to sit on, so it holds up small.
  • 3 · Bubble + N. The circle is removed, leaving the teardrop and the N. Because the N was white and the teardrop is white, the N had to be recoloured to #141414 to stay visible — otherwise it would vanish. Two shapes, no middle layer.
  • 4 · Y variant. The captain's mark — the letterform for Yoro. Same construction as option 3: white teardrop #FFFFFF plus a dark letterform #141414, two shapes, no middle layer. The Y is drawn in the N's hand — 9.2-unit monoline weight, 4.6-unit rounded-cap terminals, the same ink box as the N (x 35.7→77.5, y 32.44→83), so cap height, weight and optical centring carry over unchanged when the N is swapped out.
  • 5 · D mark. Supplied as-is. Two filled paths in near-black #0F0F0F on its own 0 0 141.7 141.7 box, no background tile. Unlike 1–4 it is dark, so it reads natively on paper and needs the invert on dark — the opposite of the others. It is not drawn in the N's hand: its edges are near-straight with a soft corner, and it carries none of the N's stepped junctions or irregular terminals.
  • 6 · Y cool. Supplied as-is, and it has a defect in the source. The disc is #FFFFFF and the two Y polygons are also #FFFFFF, with no mask, no knockout and no differing fill-rule — so the two merge and the file renders as a plain white circle. The Y was almost certainly meant to be subtracted from the disc, and the export lost it. Take the dark card as the truth of the file; the paper card is inverted only to make the disc visible.
  • Viewbox. The alternative, the bubble version and the Y variant are 0 0 114.9 115 and carry no background tile, so they need a dark surface beneath them. The current mark has its own tile. The supplied D uses 0 0 141.7 141.7 and the supplied Y 0 0 150 149.8 — both tileless and at their own scales, so the six are not mutually size-normalised; compare shapes, not apparent size.
  • On the paper theme marks 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 are inverted by CSS, which reverses every value. Mark 5 is the exception and is shown unpainted, because it starts dark. Worth comparing each against the dark column, since a mark can look right on one ground and wrong on the other.
  • Name-lock. Every card keeps the same nameplate text on purpose — Haziel Nablo — so the only variable across the six is the mark itself.
